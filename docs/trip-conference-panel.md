# Driver and vehicle activity conference

This change adds a read-only activity conference to the administrator portal. The touched root frontend files were compared with the initial main baseline `fa78fb4e278ae01cdca425767b12c8714b7e403f` before editing; unrelated synchronization changes are outside this patch.

The administrator's Utilização view adds Conferência, with a Lisbon calendar day and driver/plate search. It combines received completed Uber and Bolt services by confirmed driver and vehicle, compares their available times with complete form/native declarations, and indicates whether the exact plate is present in the synchronized Cartrack roster. No fixed vehicle assignment is required. The conference reads automatically when an administrator enters Utilização; re-entry refreshes a query after one minute. Rapid re-entry and in-flight reads do not duplicate requests. Explicitly applied dates/searches are retained; the default day follows Lisbon midnight. A displayed query time identifies the database read, not platform coverage or live telemetry. There is no background polling timer; the manual query button remains available. Expand “Ver serviços” on a row to inspect each completed service’s acceptance, pickup and end times alongside every compatible form/app declaration. Missing times remain “Sem registo”; the detail view uses the already received data and makes no additional requests. Partial results withhold compatible declaration details as well as counts.

First/last service records are activity observations, not clock-in/out times. A service without accepted/pickup time remains visibly missing its start, even if its finish is compatible with a declaration. Refusals, cancellations, unanswered requests and failed services are excluded from completed-service totals. A declaration without a compatible service or a service without a declaration does not establish an error or absence. This panel does not compute mileage; the existing Cartrack-only turn consultation remains separate.

Identity uses confirmed IDs, UUIDs and normalized exact plates. Contradictory identities and ambiguous UUIDs are withheld. All five datasets are paginated with an exact count, stable ordering and a 5,000-row limit per dataset. Missing/changing totals, duplicates and the cap cause a partial result; partial results never validate declarations. Receiving all database pages does not certify complete platform imports.

The day query selects records with an accepted, pickup, dropoff, finished or observed timestamp in that Lisbon day. Completed services must also have an actual service timestamp in the day; an import observation cannot move an old service into today's activity. A service spanning the entire day with no service timestamp inside it is outside this dated-record view. Midnight-crossing declarations are read with two days of padding on either side; missing older boundaries remain unconfirmed. Test shifts, administrative closures, source-change flags, open/invalid declarations and conflicts between native/form declarations cannot validate operational activity.

The controller permits reads for an administrator only, relies on existing database RLS, escapes displayed text, and clears cached and pending results on logout/account change. It calls no insert/update/delete/RPC/Edge Function. No schema, authorization policy, driver status, document, payment, assignment, shift or original import record is changed. The existing driver-only restriction in `/teste/` is preserved.

When completed form declarations overlap for the same driver or vehicle, both declarations require review and neither can validate mileage. Vehicle matching considers the vehicle ID and a nonempty normalized registration. A handover at the exact end of the first declaration allows both shifts to be assessed separately. The regression cases are in `tests/shift-mileage.test.cjs`.

CarTrack mileage requires a nonempty normalized registration at each boundary. If the entry and exit name different registrations, their odometers cannot establish a distance for the shift, even if the declarations share an internal vehicle ID.

For completed services, a form declaration stays pending when its entry plate, exit plate or registered vehicle plate contradict another available plate. If the declaration has no plate, confirmed internal IDs can still match a service. Linking to CarTrack always requires a nonempty normalized registration.

Service timestamps are checked in the order of acceptance, pickup, drop-off and finish. Equal timestamps and missing stages are allowed. A reversal is reported as inconsistent in the conference. During native shift review, a related trip with reversed times overlapping the shift is withheld from the counts and flagged for review. The import observation time is independent of this sequence.

The form register and the shift mileage view read import rows with stable ordering and exact counts. A changing or missing total, duplicate or missing row ID, or fewer rows than expected marks the form history as partial. Received identifiable rows remain visible once each, and partial form history withholds mileage even when CarTrack returned both odometer readings.

A form entry or exit with an outstanding source review flag withholds both mileage and declaration matching. Form parsing requires a nonempty driver name and registration that each resolve to one distinct internal ID. Duplicate normalized registrations belonging to different vehicles leave the vehicle unresolved for review.

For a service with a known positive duration, the declaration and service must share time before the five-minute boundary allowance can apply. A trip ending exactly at the next shift’s entry, or starting exactly at the previous shift’s exit, does not by itself link the adjacent shift or raise a driver mismatch in native review. When only closing stages are recorded at one instant (for example, an Uber arrival), the instant must fall after shift entry and may coincide with shift exit. For opening stages alone, it may coincide with shift entry and must precede exit. This prevents a lone observation from matching adjacent shifts through the five-minute allowance. Missing stages remain unrecorded.
